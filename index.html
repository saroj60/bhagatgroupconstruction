What types of projects does Bhagat Group specialize in?
We specialize in civil works for hydropower (camp facilities & buildings), commercial & hospital construction, road/highway projects, irrigation and drainage networks, and structural seismic retrofitting.
Where are your operations located across Nepal?
We operate throughout Nepal. Our Corporate Head Office is located in New Baneshwor-10, Kathmandu, with our active Branch Office in Bardibas-01, Mahottari, Madhesh Province—serving clients across Birgunj, Janakpur, Biratnagar, Dharan, Itahari, Rajbiraj, Lahan, Siraha, and all 7 provinces.

Yes, we provide cost estimation and preliminary engineering assessments for commercial, institutional, and infrastructure projects across all provinces.

Company Overview

Building Nepal's Infrastructure Future

Bhagat Group of Construction Pvt. Ltd. was incorporated on Bhadra 06, 2075 B.S. under the Private Firm Registration Act 2018. Since its establishment, the company has actively led civil engineering projects across the country, expanding its fleet of modern heavy machinery, skilled workforce, and engineering standards.

We pride ourselves on completing demanding engineering contracts on schedule and within budget. Our portfolio spans across hydropower civil facilities, municipal buildings, roadway expansion, irrigation canals, and vital structural rehabilitation.
